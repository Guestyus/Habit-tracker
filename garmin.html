What the words mean

Plain English for the numbers on this page.
Resting heart rateYour lowest steady heart rate, taken while you sleep. Lower is usually fitter. A sudden rise often means stress, poor sleep or an illness coming.
HRVHeart rate variability, the tiny changes in time between beats. Higher and steadier means your body is recovered. Garmin compares it to your own normal range.
Body batteryGarmin's energy estimate from 0 to 100. It charges while you sleep and drains with stress and activity. Ending the day near 5 means you ran on empty.
StressGarmin's 0 to 100 estimate from heart rate variability. Under 25 is resting, over 50 is high. It cannot tell good stress from bad.
Sleep scoreGarmin's 0 to 100 rating of last night from length, stages, restlessness and stress.
ReadinessThis page's own 0 to 100 score from four signals: HRV, resting heart rate, sleep score and body battery, each against your own last 30 days. It is not Garmin's.
Intensity minutesMinutes of moderate effort, with vigorous minutes counted twice. Health guidelines suggest 150 a week.
Training loadSession minutes weighted by how hard your heart worked. The ratio compares the last 7 days with the last 28. Around 1 is steady, well above 1.3 is a spike.
Sleep debtHours short of your sleep target, added up over the last 7 nights.
